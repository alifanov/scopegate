## 5 Recommendations

### 1. Fix or diagnose the post-registration drop-off — Priority: **Critical**
**Metric**: 7 registered, 1 logged in (14% conversion)
**Action**: Check if magic link emails are being delivered (only 1 `magic_link_sent` for 7 signups suggests either the email isn't sent or the event isn't fired). Test the full signup → magic link email → login flow manually. Check email delivery logs in Resend.
**Impact**: Fixing this could turn 6 lost users into active ones and dramatically improve activation rate.

### 2. Add product-interior event tracking — Priority: **High**
**Metric**: Zero visibility into what happens after login
**Action**: Instrument `project_created`, `endpoint_created`, `service_connected` (Google OAuth connection), `api_key_generated`, and `mcp_request_made` events with PostHog `posthog.capture()`.
**Impact**: Without these, it's impossible to measure activation, retention, or product-market fit. All funnel analysis after login is blind.

### 3. Retarget HN visitors with direct follow-up — Priority: **High**
**Metric**: 14 unique visitors on HN launch day, 7 signups total — but only 1 active user
**Action**: Review Resend logs for the 7 registered users. Send a direct "did you get stuck?" email to anyone who registered but never logged in. Offer a 15-min onboarding call.
**Impact**: Could recover 3-4 users who had intent but hit a friction point.

### 4. Add tracking for magic link click — Priority: **Medium**
**Metric**: Can't distinguish "email not delivered" vs "email not clicked" vs "link expired"
**Action**: Fire a `magic_link_clicked` event when the user follows the link back to the app. Cross-reference with `magic_link_sent` to find the drop-off point.
**Impact**: Pinpoints exactly where post-registration loss happens, enabling a targeted fix.

### 5. Improve CTA-to-signup flow — Priority: **Medium**
**Metric**: 4 users clicked CTA but 3 more signed up (suggesting some signed up without clicking tracked CTA)
**Action**: Ensure all signup entry points (CTA button, nav links, pricing page CTAs) fire `landing_cta_clicked` before redirecting. Add a second below-the-fold CTA on the homepage.
**Impact**: Better conversion attribution and potentially higher signup rate from the next traffic spike.
