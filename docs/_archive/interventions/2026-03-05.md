## 5 Recommendations

### 1. Landing page does not convert HN traffic — Priority: **Critical**
**Metric**: 31 unique users on Mar 3-5 (HN traffic), 0 signups
**Action**: The landing page is failing to convert developer traffic. Review the page against HN visitor expectations: (a) add a live demo or screenshot walkthrough visible above the fold, (b) add a "Try it now" CTA that leads to a no-auth sandbox, (c) clarify the value prop in developer-specific language (MCP, permission scoping). Consider A/B testing with a more technical landing page.
**Impact**: Even converting 10% of HN traffic = 3+ new signups per day during the traffic window.

### 2. Fix the CTA → signup disconnect — Priority: **Critical**
**Metric**: 0% ordered CTA→signup conversion; all signups bypass the CTA
**Action**: Audit where all 7 signups actually came from (likely nav bar "Login" or direct URL). Either: (a) make the main CTA button redirect to `/login` directly (not a separate flow), or (b) track all entry points to signup. The current CTA click fires `landing_cta_clicked` but doesn't actually lead users to sign up.
**Impact**: Unblocks the primary conversion path on the homepage.

### 3. Fix post-registration drop-off — Priority: **Critical** (recurring)
**Metric**: 7 registered, 1 logged in (14%), unchanged since last report
**Action**: This is now 4+ days old. Check Resend email logs immediately: are magic link emails being delivered? If yes, are they going to spam? If emails work, the magic link UX itself may be confusing. Consider adding password-based login as an alternative.
**Impact**: 6 users with signup intent are permanently lost unless re-engaged.

### 4. Add product-interior event tracking — Priority: **High** (recurring)
**Metric**: Zero visibility into post-login activity
**Action**: Instrument `project_created`, `endpoint_created`, `service_connected`, `api_key_generated`. Without this, you can't measure activation even if the login problem is fixed.
**Impact**: Required to measure product-market fit and understand what (if anything) users do after login.

### 5. Send a personal follow-up to registered users — Priority: **High** (recurring)
**Metric**: 6 users registered but never logged in, now 3-4 days cold
**Action**: Pull the 6 registered-but-not-logged-in user emails from the database. Send a personal email from the founder asking if they hit an issue. Include a direct login link. Every day that passes makes recovery less likely.
**Impact**: Could recover 2-3 users who had genuine intent but hit email/UX friction.
