## 5 Recommendations

### 1. Fix the CTA → signup flow IMMEDIATELY — Priority: **Critical** (recurring, day 6)
**Metric**: 7 CTA clicks → 0 signups through CTA path. 6 days unfixed.
**Action**: The CTA fires `landing_cta_clicked` but doesn't navigate to `/login` or `/signup`. Verify the CTA button's href/onClick handler and fix it. 2 new signups came via direct URL, proving the signup flow itself works — only the CTA routing is broken.
**Impact**: Every visitor who clicks CTA hits a dead end. This is wasting the little traffic you have.

### 2. Launch paid acquisition now — Priority: **Critical** (recurring, day 3)
**Metric**: DAU = 2, WAU dropped 22% in one day (49→38). Near-zero organic traffic.
**Action**: Start a Google Ads campaign: $10-15/day targeting "MCP proxy", "AI agent permissions", "AI access control". Even 5-10 clicks/day restarts the data pipeline for testing improvements.
**Impact**: Without consistent traffic, you can't measure if fixes work. Every day without traffic is a day of zero learning.

### 3. Fix post-registration onboarding — new signups also dropping off — Priority: **Critical** (recurring, day 6)
**Metric**: 8/9 registered users (89%) never logged in, including the 2 new signups.
**Action**: (a) Check Resend logs — are magic link emails being delivered? (b) Add an immediate redirect to dashboard after registration instead of relying on email. (c) Consider adding password auth as alternative to magic links. (d) Add a "check your email" page with resend button.
**Impact**: 89% drop-off at registration means your signup flow is fundamentally broken. Fixing this before spending on ads is essential.

### 4. Add product-interior event tracking — Priority: **High** (recurring, day 6)
**Metric**: Zero post-login visibility. No `project_created`, `endpoint_created`, `service_connected`, `api_key_generated`, `magic_link_clicked` events.
**Action**: Instrument these events in the codebase. At minimum: `magic_link_clicked`, `project_created`, `endpoint_created`, `service_connected`.
**Impact**: Required for any meaningful activation/retention analysis. Without this, you're flying blind on product usage.

### 5. Submit sitemap and request indexing in Google Search Console — Priority: **Medium** (recurring, day 2)
**Metric**: 32+ programmatic SEO pages have zero traffic after 2 days. Only 2 Google pageviews total in 30 days.
**Action**: Verify sitemap submission in GSC. Request indexing for top 5 pages manually. Add internal links from homepage to key SEO pages to help crawl discovery.
**Impact**: Organic traffic is the only sustainable source. Won't help this week but critical for next month.

Slight improvement — DAU back to 2 after two days at zero, and 2 new signups appeared. But the core problems are unchanged: CTA button is still broken (day 6), 89% of signups never log in, WAU is in freefall (-22%), and there's no organic traffic. The 2 new signups repeat the exact same drop-off pattern as the original cohort, confirming this is a systemic onboarding issue, not a cohort-specific one. **Top priorities remain: (1) fix CTA, (2) fix post-registration flow, (3) launch paid ads.**
