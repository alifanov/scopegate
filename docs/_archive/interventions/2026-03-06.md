## 5 Recommendations

### 1. The HN window is closing — act on the landing page NOW — Priority: **Critical**
**Metric**: 36 unique users since Mar 3, 0 signups. Traffic declining daily (14 → 12 → 6 → 4).
**Action**: The HN traffic is nearly gone. If you haven't already: (a) add a compelling demo video or interactive walkthrough above the fold, (b) add a "Try without signup" or sandbox mode, (c) rethink the page for developer audiences who need to see value before creating an account. This is the most urgent action — every day without changes wastes the long-tail HN traffic.
**Impact**: The remaining HN long-tail traffic (aggregators like hackernewz, reddit, stumblevibe) will dry up within days.

### 2. Fix the CTA button — it's a dead end — Priority: **Critical** (recurring, day 3)
**Metric**: 7 total CTA clicks, 0 conversions through the CTA path, 3 days unfixed
**Action**: The CTA button fires `landing_cta_clicked` but does NOT lead users to sign up. Verify what the CTA button actually does — does it scroll to a section? Open a modal? Whatever it does, it should redirect to `/login` or `/signup`. This is the #1 conversion blocker on the homepage.
**Impact**: 12.8% of visitors click the CTA — if even half of those converted, that's 3-4 more signups.

### 3. Rescue the 6 registered-but-not-logged-in users — Priority: **Critical** (recurring, day 3)
**Metric**: 6/7 registered users never logged in, now 4-5 days cold
**Action**: These users are going cold. Query the database for their emails NOW and send a personal founder email. Every additional day reduces recovery probability. Also check Resend email delivery logs — if magic link emails aren't being delivered, this is the root cause.
**Impact**: Could recover 2-3 users with genuine intent. After 7 days, recovery probability drops below 5%.

### 4. Add product-interior event tracking — Priority: **High** (recurring, day 3)
**Metric**: Zero post-login visibility
**Action**: Instrument `project_created`, `endpoint_created`, `service_connected`, `api_key_generated`, `magic_link_clicked`. Without these, even if you fix the login/signup issues, you won't know if users find value in the product.
**Impact**: Required for any meaningful activation and retention analysis.

### 5. Set up retargeting and re-engagement — Priority: **Medium**
**Metric**: 56 unique visitors, 89% homepage bounce, declining traffic
**Action**: (a) Add a simple email capture (e.g., "Get notified when we launch X") for visitors not ready to sign up. (b) Set up Google Ads retargeting pixel for future campaigns. (c) Post a follow-up on HN with a demo or case study once the product is more polished.
**Impact**: Captures intent from visitors who aren't ready to commit but might return later.
