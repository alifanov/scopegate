## 5 Recommendations

### 1. Launch paid ads TODAY — this is now day 2 at zero traffic — Priority: **Critical** (recurring, day 2)
**Metric**: DAU = 0 for 2 consecutive days. Zero organic traffic.
**Action**: Set up a Google Ads campaign targeting "MCP proxy", "AI agent permissions", "AI access control" keywords. Even $10/day budget gives you 5-15 clicks to restart the data pipeline. Without traffic, you cannot test or improve anything.
**Impact**: Every day at 0 visitors is a day of zero learning. This is the single biggest blocker.

### 2. Fix the CTA button BEFORE ads go live — Priority: **Critical** (recurring, day 5)
**Metric**: 7 CTA clicks → 0 conversions through CTA path. 5 days unfixed.
**Action**: The CTA fires `landing_cta_clicked` but doesn't navigate to `/login` or `/signup`. Fix the CTA href/onClick handler. If you launch ads before fixing this, you'll waste most of your ad budget.
**Impact**: Currently 87% of landing page visitors who engage hit a dead end.

### 3. Send rescue emails to dormant signups — LAST CHANCE — Priority: **Critical** (recurring, day 5)
**Metric**: 6/7 registered users never logged in. Now 6-7 days cold.
**Action**: Query the database for their emails, send a personal founder email TODAY. After 7 days, recovery drops below 5%. Also check Resend delivery logs to see if magic link emails were even delivered.
**Impact**: Last realistic chance to recover 1-2 users from the only cohort you have. After today, this cohort is effectively lost.

### 4. Add product-interior event tracking — Priority: **High** (recurring, day 5)
**Metric**: Zero post-login visibility. No `project_created`, `endpoint_created`, `service_connected`, `api_key_generated`, `magic_link_clicked` events.
**Action**: Instrument these events so you have activation data when traffic resumes.
**Impact**: Required for any meaningful activation/retention analysis.

### 5. Submit sitemap to Google Search Console — Priority: **Medium**
**Metric**: 32 programmatic SEO pages have zero traffic after 1 day.
**Action**: Verify sitemap is submitted in Google Search Console. Request indexing for the most important pages. Check back in 1-2 weeks. Also add internal links from the homepage to key SEO pages.
**Impact**: Long-term organic traffic play. Won't help immediately but essential for sustainable growth.
