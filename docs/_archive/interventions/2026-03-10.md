## 5 Recommendations

### 1. Launch paid ads TODAY — Priority: **Critical** (recurring, day 4)
**Metric**: DAU = 0 for 2 consecutive days. WAU declining 8%/day. Zero organic traffic.
**Action**: Start Google Ads: $10-15/day targeting "MCP proxy", "AI agent permissions", "AI access control API". Even 5-10 clicks/day breaks the deadlock.
**Impact**: Nothing else matters without traffic. You cannot test fixes to CTA, onboarding, or activation if nobody visits the site. This is now the single highest-priority action.

### 2. Fix CTA → signup flow — Priority: **Critical** (recurring, day 7)
**Metric**: 7 CTA clicks → 0 signups via CTA path. 7 days unfixed.
**Action**: The CTA button fires `landing_cta_clicked` but doesn't route to `/login` or `/signup`. Inspect the CTA's onClick/href handler and fix routing. All 9 signups came via direct navigation, proving signup works — only the CTA link is broken.
**Impact**: When traffic arrives (via ads), every CTA click will be wasted until this is fixed. Fix before spending on ads.

### 3. Replace magic link auth with immediate login — Priority: **Critical** (recurring, day 7)
**Metric**: 89% post-registration drop-off (8/9 never log in). Only 1 magic_link_sent in 30 days.
**Action**: (a) Check if magic link emails are actually being delivered (verify Resend logs). (b) Add password-based auth as primary method. (c) Auto-redirect to dashboard after registration. (d) At minimum, add a "check your email" interstitial with resend button.
**Impact**: The signup-to-login funnel is where the most users are lost. Even if ads bring traffic and CTA is fixed, 89% will still churn at this step.

### 4. Direct outreach to the 9 existing signups — Priority: **High** (new)
**Metric**: 9 registered users, 8 never returned. These are real humans who showed interest.
**Action**: Send a personal email from your address (not transactional): "Hi, I noticed you signed up for ScopeGate. Did you have trouble logging in? I'd love to help — reply to this email." This costs nothing and can recover even 1-2 users for qualitative feedback.
**Impact**: Even one reply reveals whether the problem is email delivery, magic link UX, or lack of product interest. Worth more than any analytics fix right now.

### 5. Submit sitemap + request indexing in GSC — Priority: **Medium** (recurring, day 3)
**Metric**: 32+ SEO pages with zero traffic after 3 days.
**Action**: Verify sitemap submission in Google Search Console. Manually request indexing for top 5 pages. Add internal links from homepage to key SEO pages.
**Impact**: Won't help this week but necessary for any organic pipeline.
